Exit Options
Option A — Preferred
Operator Buyout
Exercisable after Month 18. Buyout price = 18× trailing 6-month monthly average distribution.

Example: $8,000/mo avg → Buyout = $144,000
Option B
Stake Transfer
Investor may transfer their 60% revenue share to a qualified third party after Month 12, subject to operator approval. Operator retains right of first refusal.
Option C
Agreed Wind-Down
By mutual written agreement only. All business assets liquidated and proceeds distributed 60% investor / 40% operator after outstanding obligations settled.
