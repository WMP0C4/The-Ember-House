The Ember House is a first-of-its-kind private retreat and short-term rental space in rural Ontario, purpose-built to serve the province’s growing Ethical Non-Monogamy (ENM), Polyamory, and Kink communities. The business offers two primary revenue streams — curated weekend group retreats and private weekday room bookings — with workshop and education events as a third upside stream.

The venue features a fully outfitted lower-level play space alongside five private rooms appointed with bespoke custom-fabricated steel bed frames with integrated bondage attachment points. The upper level is designed as a warm social lounge. The space operates under a strict consent-first framework, serving vetted consenting adults aged 21 and over exclusively.

This document is prepared for prospective investors and presents the business model, market opportunity, capital requirements, operational framework, and three-year financial projections.
