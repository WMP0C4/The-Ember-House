Guest Conduct, Consent & Safety Framework
The Ember House — Version 1.0 — Effective 2026 — Orangeville–Caledon–Amaranth, Ontario

The Ember House is a private, members-only retreat and play space built on a foundation of radical consent, mutual respect, and personal responsibility. Every person who enters this space — guest or operator — is bound by the principles set out in this document.

This is not a list of rules imposed from the outside. These are the values that make The Ember House possible. Without them, this space cannot exist. With them, it becomes something rare and extraordinary: a place where people can explore freely, safely, and with full dignity intact.

All guests must read, understand, and sign the Acknowledgement section of this document before accessing the property. This document is distributed at time of booking confirmation and reviewed verbally by the operator at check-in for all retreat events.

The Ember House operator reserves the right to remove any guest from the property, at any time, without refund, for any breach of these standards.

Section 01
Core Principles
Everything in this document flows from these five foundational commitments. When in doubt, return here.
1
Consent Is Everything

Every interaction at The Ember House requires the enthusiastic, ongoing, and revocable consent of all parties involved. Consent given once does not mean consent given always. Consent can be withdrawn at any moment, for any reason, with no explanation required. If you are unsure whether you have consent, you do not have it.

2
No Means No. Stop Means Stop.

The words “no,” “stop,” “wait,” and any established safeword must be honoured immediately and without question. Continuing any activity after a clear stop signal constitutes a serious violation and will result in immediate removal from the property.

3
Personal Responsibility

Every guest is responsible for their own physical and emotional wellbeing, and for the impact of their actions on others. Know your limits. Communicate them clearly. Check in with your partners. This space only works when everyone takes ownership of their participation.

4
Dignity at All Times

Every person in this space deserves to be treated with dignity — during play, between scenes, in the lounge, and on departure. Judgment, mockery, or unsolicited commentary about another person’s body, preferences, or choices will not be tolerated.

5
Discretion Is Sacred

The identities, activities, and attendance of all guests are strictly private. What you see here, who you see here, and what happens here does not leave this property in any form — verbally, photographically, or digitally. Breach of this principle is the most serious violation possible and will result in permanent removal and potential legal action.

Access & Entry
Who may enter, how access is granted, and what is required before a guest sets foot on the property.

Age Requirement

Strict 21+ Policy — No Exceptions

The Ember House is exclusively available to adults aged 21 and over. Government-issued photo identification is required at check-in for every guest, every visit — regardless of prior attendance. There are no exceptions to this policy under any circumstances.

Booking & Verification

  • All bookings made through kinkbnb.com or private community channels approved by the operator
  • Access is by invitation and booking only — walk-ins are not permitted under any circumstances
  • Guests must provide their full legal name at time of booking
  • The operator reserves the right to decline or cancel any booking at their sole discretion, with a full refund issued
  • Booking confirmation constitutes agreement to these Standards of Practice

Check-In Protocol

  • All guests must check in with the operator before accessing any area of the property
  • Government-issued ID verified and recorded at check-in
  • Signed Acknowledgement form collected or confirmed digitally prior to arrival
  • For retreat events: verbal orientation covering safewords, consent protocols, and property layout conducted before the event begins
  • Guests who arrive appearing significantly intoxicated will not be admitted and will forfeit their booking fee

Guest Responsibility for Companions

Each booking guest is responsible for ensuring that all companions they bring have read, understood, and agreed to these Standards of Practice. Introducing an unbriefed guest is a violation by the booking guest.

Section 03
Consent Framework
The detailed consent principles that govern all interactions at The Ember House, inside and outside the play space.

The FRIES Model

F
Freely Given

Consent must be given freely, without pressure, manipulation, coercion, or intoxication. Creating social pressure — however subtle — to participate in any activity is a violation of this framework.

R
Reversible

Consent can be withdrawn at any time, mid-scene, mid-sentence, or after the fact. A person changing their mind does not owe anyone an explanation. The moment consent is withdrawn, all activity stops immediately.

I
Informed

Consent requires honest communication about what is being proposed. Misrepresenting an activity, withholding relevant information, or escalating beyond what was agreed constitutes a violation — even if the original activity was consented to.

E
Enthusiastic

We are looking for an enthusiastic yes — not the absence of no. Silence, ambivalence, or reluctant agreement is not consent. Check in with your partners. Ask. Communicate.

S
Specific

Consent to one activity is not consent to all activities. Consent to play with one person is not consent to play with others. Consent given previously does not carry over to new activities or participants without explicit agreement.

Negotiation Before Play

Discuss Before You Begin

All guests are strongly encouraged to negotiate the terms of any play before entering the play space — limits, safewords, experience levels, and activities desired or off-limits. The upper level social lounge exists in part to provide a comfortable, low-pressure environment for these conversations.

Intoxication & Consent

Intoxicated Persons Cannot Consent

A person who is significantly intoxicated cannot give meaningful consent. Initiating sexual or BDSM activity with a person who is visibly impaired is a serious violation and may constitute a criminal offence under Canadian law. If you are unsure whether someone is too intoxicated to consent — they are. Do not proceed.

Approaching Other Guests

  • Ask before touching — always, without exception
  • Accept rejection graciously — a “no” is complete, do not negotiate or persist
  • Do not interrupt an active scene without explicit invitation from all parties
  • Observing an active scene is not an invitation to join
  • If you are unsure whether an approach is welcome, it is not

Section 04
Safewords & Signals
The Ember House uses the universal traffic light system as its default safeword protocol. These words are posted throughout the play space.
RED
Full Stop
Everything stops immediately. No questions, no negotiation. All activity ceases and all restraints are removed as quickly as safely possible.
YELLOW
Slow Down / Check In
Something needs attention. Slow or pause the activity. Check in with your partner. The scene may continue once both parties confirm readiness.
GREEN
All Good / Continue
Explicit signal that everything is good and the scene may continue or escalate. Use this when your partner checks in with you.

Non-Verbal Signals

When Verbal Safewords Are Not Possible

In scenes involving gags, hoods, or other verbal restrictions, guests must negotiate a non-verbal alternative before beginning.

  • Hand signal: Three rapid finger taps on the partner’s body
  • Object drop: Holding a small object that when dropped signals stop
  • Vocal pattern: Three distinct grunts or vocalizations agreed upon in advance

Honouring a Safeword

Zero Tolerance

Ignoring or dismissing a safeword is a zero-tolerance violation. If engaged in consensual non-consent (CNC) or edge play with scripted objections, an unambiguous out-of-role safeword must be established before beginning. Any guest observed dismissing a safeword will be removed immediately, permanently, and without refund.

Section 05
Guest Conduct
Expectations for behaviour throughout the property — in the social lounge, the play space, private rooms, and everywhere in between.

What Is Welcome Here

  • Enthusiastic, negotiated, consensual play between adults aged 21+
  • BDSM, kink, and power exchange activities that all involved parties have agreed to
  • Group play in the designated play space during retreat events — with consent of all participants
  • Observation of open scenes with implicit or explicit permission from participants
  • Socialising and building community in the upper level lounge
  • Responsible alcohol consumption — drinking is welcome, incapacitation is not
  • Open, honest conversations about desires, limits, and boundaries

What Is Not Permitted

  • Photography or video recording of any kind — anywhere on the property, at any time, without exception
  • Non-consensual touching of any kind
  • Solicitation or exchange of money for sexual services on the premises
  • Bringing any guest who has not been verified, booked, and agreed to these Standards of Practice
  • Sharing the address or any identifying information about this property with anyone not already a confirmed guest
  • Harassment, repeated unwanted approach, or failure to accept a “no”
  • Use of illegal substances on the premises
  • Any activity involving minors — zero tolerance, immediate removal and law enforcement notification

Zero Tolerance for Recording

Phones must be stored face-down or in pockets/bags in all play areas and the upper-level designated private rooms during retreat events. Any guest observed photographing or recording others without explicit permission will be removed immediately and permanently.

Section 06
Hygiene & Sanitization
A clean, safe physical environment is as important as a safe social one. These standards protect every guest equally.

Personal Hygiene

Guests are expected to arrive freshly showered and in good personal hygiene. Guests who are noticeably unhygienic may be asked to use the shower facilities before accessing the play space.

Barrier Methods

Safer Sex Supplies Available Throughout the Property

Condoms, gloves, and dental dams are provided in all play areas and private rooms. Guests are strongly encouraged to use barrier methods consistent with their own risk assessment and prior agreements with their partners.

Equipment Use & Care

  • All shared play space equipment must be wiped down with the provided surface sanitizer before and after each use
  • Leather restraint equipment must be wiped with the provided leather-safe cleaner after each use
  • Report any equipment damage to the operator immediately
  • All upholstered surfaces are treated by the operator with Micrylium BioText as part of post-event cleaning protocols

Private Room Protocol

  • Fresh linens provided for every booking — do not reuse linens from a previous session
  • The waterproof mattress liner must remain on the mattress at all times
  • Strip used linens and leave them in the provided laundry bag at end of session
  • Leave the room in reasonable condition — the operator will complete full sanitization between bookings

Section 07
Incidents & Response
How The Ember House handles consent violations, medical situations, and guest removal.

Reporting a Concern

Any guest who witnesses or experiences a consent violation or any situation that makes them feel unsafe is encouraged to bring it to the operator immediately. You will be taken seriously, treated with discretion, and no retaliation will be tolerated. During retreat events, a designated Consent Monitor is present on-site throughout.

Consent Violations — Response Protocol

1
Immediate Separation

The operator or consent monitor will immediately separate the involved parties. The reporting guest will not be asked to confront the accused party or remain in their presence.

2
Private Conversation

The operator will speak privately with the reporting guest. Notes will be taken. The reporting guest’s account is the primary record.

3
Determination & Action

In cases of clear or serious violation, the offending guest will be removed immediately — without refund and without debate. The safety and comfort of the reporting guest is the priority.

4
Permanent Ban

Any guest removed for a consent violation is permanently banned from The Ember House. This decision is final and non-negotiable.

Medical Emergencies

In Any Medical Emergency — Call 911 First

The safety of guests always supersedes the privacy of the venue. Call 911 immediately in any medical emergency. Do not delay emergency services out of concern for discretion. Nearest hospital: Headwaters Health Care Centre, Orangeville — 100 Rolling Hills Drive, approximately 10–25 minutes from the property.

Scene Distress — Drop & Subdrop

Guests experiencing emotional or psychological distress following intense play are encouraged to reach out to the operator, their partner, or the consent monitor. A quiet recovery space is available. Aftercare is expected between all play partners.
